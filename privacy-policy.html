Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Sterling Capital Advisors ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA") of Singapore. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data you provide to us through this website.

1. Personal Data We Collect

When you use our enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Investment goals and estimated investment amount
  • Any other information you choose to share with us in your message

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or automated profiling tools on this website.

2. Purposes for Collection, Use and Disclosure

We collect, use, and disclose your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your enquiries and requests for a consultation
  • Scheduling and conducting consultations with our advisors
  • Providing information about our investment advisory services tailored to your stated goals
  • Maintaining records of communications for service quality and compliance
  • Complying with our legal and regulatory obligations

3. Consent

By submitting the enquiry form on this website, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data for the purposes set out in this Policy. If you do not consent, please do not submit your personal data through this website.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We use FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form processing service, to receive and route enquiry form submissions to our team by email. If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, your messages are processed through WhatsApp/Meta Platforms in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Protection of Personal Data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data in our possession or control from unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal, or similar risks.

6. Retention of Personal Data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Access and Correction

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you by contacting us using the details in Section 10. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time and in accordance with the PDPA.

8. Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your personal data at any time by emailing us. Please note that withdrawing consent may affect our ability to respond to your enquiry or provide our services to you.

9. Do Not Call (DNC) Registry

We will only contact you by telephone, SMS, or other voice or text messages for marketing purposes if you have given clear consent to do so, in compliance with the Do Not Call (DNC) provisions under Part 9 of the PDPA. Providing your phone number through our enquiry form constitutes consent for us to contact you regarding your enquiry.

11. Changes to This Policy
